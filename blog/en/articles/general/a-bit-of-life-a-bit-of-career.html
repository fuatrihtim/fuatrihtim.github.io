A Bit of Life, A Bit of Career

It has been about 4 years since I created this platform. Not everything went as I had planned, but that was definitely not a negative thing. While I expected to focus more on things I would create myself, life brought its own priorities that required attention. Everything that developed outside my plans actually added a lot to my life.

Outside of work, I had the chance to see many places. Before even reaching the three-year mark, I managed to travel quite a lot. I drove nearly 100,000 kilometers with my car. I flew to many places. I attended trainings, spent time with my best friend, worked to become better at my job, and more. I met many interesting and great people. Even though the numbers may differ when it comes to collecting quality people in my life, I can say that in 3 years I have come much further than I did in the previous 33 years.

During this period, the stress caused by things beyond our control was much less compared to the previous 33 years. Because of that, I managed to keep my focus even in the most difficult situations. Still, I can say that it was the most challenging period of my life. When I was in Turkiye, I never avoided taking responsibility in any environment/community/group I was part of. After coming to the Netherlands, even though I was proactive and familiar with many things, and even though many wonderful people helped me, adaptation takes time. After going through that process, I returned to my natural default and started pushing for more again. I encountered responsibilities I did not expect, and I did not step back.

It has been about 4.5 years since I started life from scratch. No matter how much we try to appreciate the moment we live in, the awareness of what really happened becomes clearer over time. When I look back, the only thing I can say is this: it was very difficult, and I have come a long way. The difficulty did not come from defeat or change. The baggage of the past made everything mentally and physically harder. There are only a few steps left before I can say, “it is all behind me.” The road was difficult and long, but when I look back while writing this, I see that I have accomplished quite a lot.

There have also been serious changes in my career. I changed many companies, met many people, and developed hundreds of projects or products in the past. Everywhere I went, I worked day and night to create value. But it was not only about working hard; I also kept my eyes on the world at all times. I tried to learn from the best and what they do right, and from the mistakes of the worst, and apply those lessons to every day of my life. No one has a teacher standing beside them 24 hours a day teaching everything.

On top of all this, I tried to use a trait of mine—something I consider good but that has also caused me a lot of harm—in a better way. Even though in my personal life it sometimes keeps me away from situations I would like to be in, I do not hold back from it because it is something that makes me who I am. Many sad things have happened, but if there is one thing that makes me valuable or successful, I will never give up this trait. I am talking about thinking through and calculating all possibilities, even in the simplest situations. This is not just about coding. Many of the problems we face come from missing pieces in the algorithms we build. Empathy helps us build good relationships and overcome problems, but most of the time it does not help us solve them at the root. We spend our limited lives in this world struggling with the same problems over and over again. Many people interpret this as perfectionism, but my only goal has always been zero problems and zero negative surprises. That is why I try to consider every possibility in every line of code I write and in every human relationship I have. It happens naturally; I do not make a special effort for it, but it has been mentally exhausting. For many years now, the periods when I could sleep comfortably—or even just sleep—have been very limited.

Coding… For the first time, I struggled this much, but I also learned a lot. For example, I tried to learn in detail the things that most people avoid. Thankfully, the best team I have ever worked with contributed a lot to this. They patiently helped me. I am still not at the point I want to reach, but when I think about the responsibilities I have taken on now… We have done very good work. This is the first time I find myself using the plural form. In the past, I experienced situations where everything was expected from a single person and I could not find people who would support me. Now I have an incredible team. Their responsibility is on my shoulders. In practice, I am not doing something completely different. The extra things I used to do while coding in previous years are now officially my full responsibility.

A monster team! They are truly interesting people, and I love their differences. They have such strong sides that when we set off in the right direction, with the right plan and at the right time, they accomplish incredible things. For the first time, I do not feel anxious. Are there missing parts? Do we make mistakes? Of course. But for the first time, I am not worried. For the first time, I am not saying “only I can save this situation again.” It is a huge luxury. I love them all—not because of what they can do, but because what they say, what they do, and who they are align with each other. For the first time, I see such a group and such a level of trust. Despite many difficulties, I see loyalty to the plan and trust. This pushes me to do more for their happiness: so they can have a better work-life balance, produce higher-quality work, and achieve more. And that is not all. They are incredibly creative. When given even a small opportunity, they produce things that leave people amazed. Incredible. I am working hard to make this vision our shared vision. When the right days come, I have no doubt they will have the things they dream of. Most importantly, even in the hardest situations, we can still joke around and smile. What more could I ask for?

Goals… They never end. I am very close to one of my biggest goals. When I achieve it, I can imagine the day when I will be able to think of the day closing my eyes “peacefully” forever. Whatever happens after that, nothing will be a big deal to me. I think within about 1–1.5 years I will be able to clarify it. Maybe in my next article I will talk about it and share my happiness. Who knows?

The work side is a bit difficult, but I will succeed—we will succeed. In a way that no one expects. My biggest goal in this matter is that after everything is accomplished, instead of boasting, I will give all the credit to the real heroes of this work. I want to see their happiness and see their ideas and values recognized more. Personally, I have no expectations or concerns. If something is meant to happen and is possible, I will find a way to make it happen. Thankfully, until today I have never failed in this matter. But this time the responsibility is much greater. We will do it anyways.

I used to want to see many places, but right now I do not even feel like traveling. I have many things to accomplish in the next year. Not only for myself, but for many people. Still, I have not given up on that goal. Maybe in between everything I can make some beautiful and long travel plans. After taming the mental difficulties, perhaps during those trips I can isolate myself from everything. Maybe I will meet new people and spend some pleasant time.

These are somewhat difficult days. Hard to carry. But they will pass…
